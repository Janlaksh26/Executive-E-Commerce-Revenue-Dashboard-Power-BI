##Executive E-Commerce & Revenue Dashboard##

Technology Used: Power BI
Problem Statement
Raw transactional sales data can make it difficult for business users to quickly understand revenue, profitability, customer contribution, regional performance, delivery performance, and payment trends.
The objective of this project is to transform transactional sales data into an interactive business intelligence dashboard that provides a consolidated view of key business metrics.
Data Preparation
Superstore Sales or Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset (available on Kaggle).
The dataset was imported into Power BI and prepared using Power Query.
The preparation process included reviewing:
•	Data types
•	Numerical fields
•	Date fields
•	Categorical fields
•	Sales and profit values
•	Delivery information
•	Customer and order information
Data Analysis
DAX measures were developed to calculate important KPIs and support dynamic analysis.
The dashboard can be filtered using Region and Category slicers.
Dashboard Components: 
KPI Section
•	Total Revenue
•	Total Profit
•	Total Orders
•	Average Delivery Days
Sales Trend
Analyzes sales performance over time and includes a forecast of future sales.
Customer Performance
Compares customer sales, profit, and order volume.
Category & Region
Compares category-level sales across different regions.
Delivery Performance
Analyzes average delivery days by state.
Customer Segment
Shows sales contribution from different customer segments.
Payment Mode
Analyzes sales distribution across different payment modes.
Expected Business Use
The dashboard can support business users in monitoring sales performance, understanding customer behavior, comparing regions and categories, and identifying areas requiring further analysis.
